# Volume 3 — Section I: Safety, Health and Environment (SHE) Manual

This volume sets out the mandatory Safety, Health, and Environment (SHE) requirements for contractors executing demolition and dismantling works under BMRCL Tender No. `BMRCL/Phase-3/JPN-MRS/Demolition/DLN-P1/2026/157`.

* **[Part I: SHE Management (vol3_part1.md)](vol3_part1.md)** — General provisions, targets, compliance, contractor SHE policy/plan, designer's role, organisation, committee, induction, training, inspection, audit, communication, submittals, accident reporting, and emergency plans (Clauses 1–16).
* **[Part II: Safety (vol3_part2.md)](vol3_part2.md)** — Housekeeping, working at height, overhead protection, slipping/tripping hazards, lifting appliances, construction machinery, site electricity, lighting, tools, welding, demolition, excavation, work permits, traffic management, and PPEs (Clauses 17–40).
* **[Part III: Occupational Health and Welfare (vol3_part3.md)](vol3_part3.md)** — Occupational health, medical examinations, drinking water, sanitation, first aid, and welfare facilities (Clauses 41–46).
* **[Part IV: Environmental Management (vol3_part4.md)](vol3_part4.md)** — Air quality, dust suppression, noise control, waste management, and environmental protection (Clauses 47–51).
* **[Part V: Penalty and Awards (vol3_part5.md)](vol3_part5.md)** — Safety violation penalties, penalty schedule, and safety incentive awards (Clauses 52–53).
* **[Appendices, General Instructions and Forms (vol3_appendices_instructions_forms.md)](vol3_appendices_instructions_forms.md)** — MOUs, BOCW Act compliance, Site SHE Plan, HIV/AIDS policy, CEO general instructions for manpower, training, lighting, signage, and statutory SHE committee forms.
