# Notice Inviting Tender

This section contains the Notice Inviting Tender (NIT) for BMRCL Tender No. `BMRCL/Phase-3/JPN-MRS/Demolition/DLN-P1/2026/157`, dated 09.09.2026, concerning partial and full demolition or dismantling of buildings and structures identified for Bangalore Metro Rail Project Phase 3.

The NIT describes the invitation to tender, key commercial details, tender dates, participation requirements and submission instructions for Contract Package P1, covering JP Nagar 4th Phase Station to Mysore Road Station, from Ch: (-) 0.330 Km to Ch: 10.550 Km in Corridor-1.

## A. Tender Invitation

### Invitation to Tender

<!-- Source: PDF page 7; NIT booklet page 001 -->

- **Inviting Organisation:** Bangalore Metro Rail Corporation Limited (BMRCL), a Joint Venture of the Government of Karnataka and the Government of India.
- **Tendering Method:** Open e-tenders, Single stage – Two Cover System.
- **Publication Portal:** CPP Portal, https://etenders.gov.in/eprocure/app.
- **Eligible Applicants:** Applicants fulfilling the prescribed [[Vol1/secC|Eligibility cum Qualification Criteria]] (EQC).
- **Project Scope:** Partial and Full Demolition/Dismantling of Buildings/Structures identified in Bangalore Metro Rail Project Phase-3.
- **Contract Package:** P1.
