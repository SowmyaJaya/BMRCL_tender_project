# General Conditions of Contract and Contract Data

This section contains the General Conditions of Contract (GCC) and the Contract Data for BMRCL Tender No. `BMRCL/Phase-3/JPN-MRS/Demolition/DLN-P1/2026/157`, concerning partial and full demolition or dismantling of buildings and structures identified for Bangalore Metro Rail Project Phase 3.

The GCC sets out the contractual rights, obligations and procedures of the Employer, the Engineer and the Contractor across 18 clauses. The Contract Data gives the project-specific values and options for selected GCC sub-clauses.

This file lists clause headings and sub-clause (x.y) headings only. Deeper numbering (for example 4.2.1 or 11.1.2) is omitted.
