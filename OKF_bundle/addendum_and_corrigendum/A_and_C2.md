### Change 1 — Variation to Quantities of Minor Value Items

- **Amends:** [[OKF_bundle/Technical/Vol1/secE#Clause 12 — Variations|GCC Clause 12 — Variations]]
- **Exact affected reference as printed:** Sub-Clause 12.3.1, (iii) c
- **Change type:** Replace the specified minor-value-item passage
- **Evidence:** `AandC2_DLN_P1_157.PDF`, PDF page 1, amendment serial 1
- **Affected booklet pages:** 121 and 122

#### Existing Wording

> Variation to quantities of Minor Value Item: The limit for varying quantities for minor value items shall be 100% (as against 25% prescribed for other items). A minor value item for this purpose is defined as an item whose original agreement value is less than 1 % of the total original contract value.
>
> (i) Quantities operated upto and in excess of 100% of the agreement quantity of the concerned minor value item, shall be paid at the rate awarded for that item as per accepted rate;
>
> (ii) Quantities operated in excess of 100% but upto 200% of the agreement quantity of the concerned minor value item, shall be paid at 98% of the rate awarded for that item as per accepted rate;
>
> (iii) Variation in quantities of individual minor value item beyond 200% shall be paid at 96% of the rate awarded for that item as per accepted rate.

#### Replacement Wording

> Variation to quantities of Minor Value Item: The limit for varying quantities for minor value items shall be 100% (as against 25% prescribed for other items). A minor value item for this purpose is defined as an item whose original agreement value is less than 1 % of the total original contract value.
>
> (i) Quantities operated upto and including 200% of the agreement quantity of the concerned minor value item, shall be paid at the rate awarded for that item as per accepted rate;
>
> (ii) Quantities operated in excess of 200% but upto 300% of the agreement quantity of the concerned minor value item, shall be paid at 98% of the rate awarded for that item as per accepted rate;
>
> (iii) Variation in quantities of individual minor value item beyond 300% shall be paid at 96% of the rate awarded for that item as per accepted rate.

#### Reading Note

The existing phrase “upto and in excess of 100%” is retained as printed. It has not been silently rewritten.

The replacement changes the payment bands to:

| Quantity relative to agreement quantity | Payment rate |
|---|---|
| Up to and including 200% | Accepted item rate |
| Above 200% and up to 300% | 98% of accepted item rate |
| Above 300% | 96% of accepted item rate |

This table is a structured reading aid. The replacement wording above remains the supporting source text.
